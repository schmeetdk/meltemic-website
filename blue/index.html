The questions

What I'll be asking.

  1. Who owns the data from an offshore asset – the operator, the OEM, the sensor vendor – and who is actually allowed to act on it?
  2. How much ocean data is collected and never trusted? For industrial time series the honest answer was "most of it." Is the sea any different?
  3. What does a port authority need from a data start-up: a dashboard, or a decision it can defend?
  4. Where is the capital going – into sensors, or into making the data from existing sensors worth believing?

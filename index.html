The name

Named for a wind you can plan around.

The Meltemi is the summer wind of the Aegean – the Etesian, from the Greek for annual. It arrives every year, from the same quarter, with the same force. Sailors don't fight it. They set a course that uses it.

That is the whole idea. Markets, capital and AI are the weather: strong, constant, and not yours to control. My work is to read them and set the course that turns them into speed.
